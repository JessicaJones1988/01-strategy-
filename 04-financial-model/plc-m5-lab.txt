## Stress-test and finalize
- **Paste your finalized business case here.:** Meridian · Field Workflow Simplification — Business Case
Strategic Bet

Meridian will simplify field workflows for foremen by streamlining daily logs and enabling push notifications for high-priority job-site issues. The goal is to reduce friction for field users, increase regular Meridian usage, and support improved customer retention and expansion.

Target outcome: Reduce the average time for a foreman to document and communicate a job-site issue from 5 minutes to under 2 minutes within two quarters.

Key Assumptions

The business case depends on three primary assumptions:

Foremen will adopt the simplified daily-log and notification workflows.
Reducing workflow time from 5 minutes to under 2 minutes will increase regular product usage.
Increased usage will contribute to improved customer retention and future expansion.

Load-bearing assumption: The most important assumption is that faster workflows will lead to higher ongoing product adoption. If the adoption impact is materially lower than expected, the expected retention and growth benefits will also be reduced.

Expected Return

At the customer level, the investment is expected to increase frequency and consistency of field usage, creating greater product engagement and supporting retention and expansion.

At scale, success will be measured across Meridian's active field customers. Within two quarters, the average time to document and communicate a job-site issue should fall from 5 minutes to under 2 minutes.

The financial case should be validated by measuring whether increased usage produces measurable improvements in customer retention, LTV, and expansion revenue.

Financial Stress Tests

Before final funding approval, the case should be tested against:

10-point increase in churn: Confirm LTV remains above the 3:1 target.
20% increase in CAC: Recalculate payback and confirm the business is still comfortable with the resulting period.
Adoption sensitivity: Evaluate the financial impact if the improvement in regular usage is 20–30% below expectations.
Kill Criterion

If the average time to complete and communicate a job-site issue is not below 2 minutes within two quarters, stop further investment and reallocate Engineering resources to higher-priority initiatives.

This criterion provides a clear metric, target, timeline, and action. The financial consequence should be further defined by establishing a specific investment or dollar threshold for continued funding.

Recommendation

FUND WITH CONDITION: Proceed with the workflow investment, subject to completing the financial model and confirming that the retention and payback economics remain acceptable under the defined stress tests.

Primary item to resolve before final approval: Quantify the connection between faster field workflows → increased usage → improved retention/expansion → financial return.
